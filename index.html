Firmware for IoT, BMS, automotive and real-time systems

I build reliable firmware for connected devices, from the first board bring-up to fleets running in the field. Over 7 years I have shipped products on ESP32, STM32 and the Nordic nRF family (nRF52, nRF5340, nRF93M1, nRF7002), writing C and C++ on FreeRTOS and Zephyr.

I specialise in battery management systems (BMS): multi-cell Li-Ion monitoring, charging control and accurate state-of-charge and state-of-health estimation on TI fuel gauges and chargers. Around that, I build devices that last on a battery, update safely over the air and stay connected over BLE, Wi-Fi, LoRa or cellular. I also bring training in AUTOSAR Classic and ISO 26262 for automotive projects.
